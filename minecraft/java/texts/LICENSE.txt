Corsu — Minecraft: Java Edition, splash texts and End Poem in Corsican (optional pack)
https://github.com/Platykalt/corsu

This pack replaces assets/minecraft/texts/splashes.txt and end.txt. Minecraft reads these files whatever language is
selected, so with this pack they are in Corsican for every language. Remove the pack to get them back.

End Poem: Corsican translation of the text by Julian Gough, who dedicated it to the public domain under CC0 1.0
(https://theeggandtherock.com/p/i-wrote-a-story-for-a-friend). Splash texts: Corsican translation of Mojang's text;
Mojang keeps its rights in the original. Corsu's own contribution is offered under the GNU GPL, version 3 or later.
