Corsu

Keyman now suggests and corrects Corsican words (Cismuntincu and Pumuntincu forms alike, no preference) as you type, with the EuroLatin (SIL) keyboard added for Corsican. Keyman uses one dictionary per language. To change to another Corsu dictionary on Android, first uninstall this one (Keyman settings, Installed Languages, Corsican, Dictionary, Uninstall dictionary); on an iPhone, choose it under Installed Languages, Corsican, Dictionaries. Report a missing or wrong word at https://github.com/Platykalt/corsu/issues.

Keyman prupone è currege avà e parolle corse mentre scrivite, cù una tastera in corsu (EuroLatin, per esempiu).
