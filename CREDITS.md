# Credits

## Translations Corsu reuses

Corsu's lexicon reuses Corsican translations published by other free software projects, under their own licences
(listed per section in `lexicon/lexicon-upstream.tsv` and `lexicon/lexicon-mozilla.tsv`):

- **Patriccollu di Santa Maria è Sichè**, author of almost all of them: Audacity, VLC, WinMerge, wxWidgets,
  Notepad++, Thunderbird for Android, fre:ac, Tenacity, Poedit, OpenTracks, Inno Setup, and many more
  ([Lingua_Corsa-Infurmatica](https://github.com/Patriccollu/Lingua_Corsa-Infurmatica)).
- **The Corsican Mozilla team** on Pontoon: Firefox for Android and iOS, Focus, Mozilla VPN (MPL-2.0).

Their texts are kept as published; Corsu's own adaptations go in `lexicon/lexicon-fixes.tsv` and are listed in
`lexicon/corrections.tsv`.

## Source texts

- **Julian Gough**, the End Poem of Minecraft, dedicated by him to the public domain (CC0 1.0); Corsu's optional
